# TIT COMP v0.6.0

Free compressor with nine modes, sidechain EQ and a Color switch.

## Download
Download the zip for your system from the **Assets** list below:
- `TITCOMP-Windows-VST3.zip`: Windows (VST3).
- `TITCOMP-macOS-VST3.zip` and `TITCOMP-macOS-AU.zip`: macOS (VST3 and AU). One universal build for Apple Silicon and Intel.
- `TITCOMP-source.zip`: source code.

Unzip it and copy `TIT COMP.vst3` to your VST3 folder (Windows: `C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3`; macOS: `/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3`, AU: `/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components`), then rescan plugins in your DAW.
Unsigned macOS builds: in Terminal run `xattr -cr "TIT COMP.vst3"` (or `.component`).

## What's in it
- Modes: Clean, VCA, FET, FET B, optical, variable-mu, mu, RMS VCA and digital.
- 10-band sidechain EQ, SC Listen, lookahead, oversampling up to 8x.
- Color switch (on by default, configurable from the menu or `config.txt`).
- Optical and variable-mu modes refitted to new measurements.

Free and open source (AGPLv3). No registration.
